Joker chance, guaranteed Joker line) and a few utilities (free spins, set hearts, free Fate Stone
rerolls) are adapted from **Slotbound OddsMod** by **TokioKaze**:
https://github.com/TokioKaze/-Slotbound-OddsMod-
